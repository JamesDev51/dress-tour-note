Offline garment artwork pack

These lossless WebP files are the optimized local copies of the approved
garment candidates and the two measured accessory masters. The runtime
registry in src/lib/renderer/garment contains their coverage, hashes, source
frames, and measured registration. Files are local photographic sources with a
calibrated near-magenta RGB plate; the renderer applies its local chroma matte
and never fetches a remote image.
